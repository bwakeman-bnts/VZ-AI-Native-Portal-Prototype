Reading this wireframe

Slot order is not page order. The Slots tab defines six slots and their fill rules, not their positions. Placing S4 and S6 in a right rail is an interpretation, and the arrangement is the thing to argue with — the fills are what the matrix determines.

Absence is not low rank. Gates 1–4 remove cards entirely. A card listed above as absent does not sit at the bottom of the page; it does not exist for this visit.
