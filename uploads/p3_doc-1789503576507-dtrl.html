Absent from this page, and why

  • Every J4 card (8) and every J10 card (7) — zero under Retain by rule. Absent, not ranked low. Do not sell past a problem. The Test Profiles tab says “fourteen at zero”; the Cards tab holds fifteen.
  • Every remaining commercial card — suppressed by the Escalated manner regardless of objective score. This catches J5-07 Change a plan (Retain 3), J2-10 Set up a package and J12-02 Start an integration, all of which would otherwise be in contention.
  • J1-11 What we can do to keep you — Retain 3, and the one offer-shaped card exempt from the Retain veto as a retention instrument. Suppressed here anyway, because Manner zeroes every commercial card and the Vetoes tab writes that exemption against the Retain rule, not against Manner. Worth confirming that reading — an offer at third contact is a gamble either way.
  • J3-08 Keep running while it is down — precondition fails. No incident affects a site on this account.

The route-card problem — only this profile exposes it

  • Four route cards are in contention on one page: J9-05 (Lead, via Manner), J9-06 (Support), J3-05 Escalate (Support-eligible, Retain 3) and J9-01 (Escape, unvetoable).
  • Nothing in the Slots tab stops three appearing at once. The only de-dup rule is S3's “may not repeat the lead's job more than once,” and J3-05 is a J3 card, so it does not collide with a J9 lead.
  • A page offering an escalated customer three different humans is worse than one offering a single obvious one. This needs a rule.

What does not change

  • The objective stays Retain — nothing in the request touches Risk or Open issue.
  • The escalation stays in slot 1 of the status strip.
  • Commercial stays suppressed. A dispute is not a buying signal.

This is where the S3 repeat rule bites. A dispute needs evidence beside it — J1-10 Payment history and J1-02 What changed this cycle are both the obvious companions, and both are J1. Reading this wireframe

Slot order is not page order. The Slots tab defines six slots and their fill rules, not their positions. Placing S4 and S6 in a right rail is an interpretation — the arrangement is what to argue with, the fills are what the matrix determines.

Absence is not low rank. Gates 1–4 remove cards entirely. Nothing listed as absent sits at the bottom of the page; it does not exist for this visit.
